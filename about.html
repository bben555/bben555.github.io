Our story

The people behind
every metta smile.

Four caregivers, two clinics, one shared belief — that denture care should feel as warm as it is precise.

“

metta
(Pali)

A Pali word for a simple, wholesome wish for others' well-being.

At Metta Denture Clinic, we take that idea seriously in a practical way: top-notch denture care, delivered in a warm, empathetic environment where every patient feels valued. It means blending advanced denturist practices with a compassionate touch, so each visit is as comfortable and reassuring as possible.

Our team

Meet the people who'll care for your smile

Wolf

Denturist & Owner

Wolf has been involved in dentistry for over 30 years and is a member of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI). He speaks Cantonese, Mandarin, and English. Wolf studied Stomatology and Prosthodontics in China, earning his Prosthodontist license in 1995, and practiced at his own clinics in Shenzhen for more than 20 years. Since coming to Canada, he completed the denturist program at CDI College and holds denturist licenses in both BC and Ontario. In his spare time, Wolf enjoys restoring antiques and art pieces, and playing clarinet.

Claire

Office Receptionist

Claire is the friendly voice you'll hear when you call, and the first face you'll see when you arrive. She speaks English and Mandarin, and is currently expanding her skills to help with simple lab work in the office. In her free time she likes to play piano and go on long walks.

Ben

Denturist-in-Training

Ben has been helping Wolf in and out of the clinic and enjoys making dentures for others so much that he's pursuing a career as a denturist — currently enrolled in the Denturism program at George Brown College in Toronto. Ben has a sharp eye for detail. In his spare time he likes going on walks, riding motorcycles, and meditating.

Kelly

Denturist-in-Training

Kelly helps care for patients at our New Westminster clinic while pursuing her denturism education. She brings a calm, patient-first approach to every appointment and loves helping first-time denture wearers feel at ease from their very first visit.
